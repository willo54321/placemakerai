=====================================================================
PER PROJECT — AI ANALYSIS (Anthropic credits)
=====================================================================

Budget ~£20 (~$25) per consultation project.

  Basis: one full AI analysis run costs roughly $0.30-0.50 on a
  typical project (~50 feedback items) and up to ~$2.50 at the
  400-item cap. Results are cached and only re-run when feedback
  changes, so a live consultation triggers a handful of paid runs
  per week. £20/project is comfortable headroom; actual spend will
  likely be £5-15.

  The $20 already loaded covers the first project entirely.
  Set a monthly spend cap in console.anthropic.com as a backstop.
  Fold the £20 into per-project client pricing.

ONE-OFF / ANNUAL — CREDIBILITY & COMPLIANCE
=====================================================================

Cyber Essentials certification   ~£320 + VAT (= £384/yr)
  Trigger: when actively pitching councils. Includes £25k cyber
  insurance. Renews annually. Half a day of prep (laptop hardening,
  asset inventory) before applying.

Cyber Essentials PLUS            ~£1,500-2,000/yr
  Trigger: ONLY if a specific contract/framework demands the audited
  version. Do not buy speculatively.

External penetration test        ~£2,000-5,000 one-off
  Trigger: ONLY when a contract requires it. Until then "internally
  security reviewed" is the honest answer.

Legal review (DPA + terms)       ~£500-1,500 one-off
  Trigger: before signing the first council contract. Drafts can be
  prepared free; a solicitor reviews rather than writes.
